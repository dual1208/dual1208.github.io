# Public catalogue schema

`catalogue.json` is the application data. `catalogue.sqlite` contains only the same sanitized public research fields, not the private research database. Neither contains source captures, cookies, account information, original source-file blobs, or private Drive links.

## Counting and scope

- 649 canonical programme records.
- 214 candidate observations, not 214 unique additional programmes. Candidate observations may revisit an award.
- 450 lead records, including negative screens and historical records. These are not recommendations and can overlap.
- Exactly 64 records are marked `latestRegional`: the October 30 Singapore/Hong Kong batch, including ten canonical refreshes and 54 candidate observations.
- One explicit pure-mathematics negative lead is flagged `pureMaths`; applied/financial mathematics and phrases saying “not pure mathematics” are not excluded.

Do not present the sum as a unique-programme count. Full historical research files remain outside this public export. Every canonical programme has at least one linked source. Thirty-nine candidate observations and 46 leads have unresolved direct URLs in the scoped archive; `sourceLinkStatus` and a displayed limitation identify these records. No URL is invented to fill a gap.

## JSON

Root `meta` records schema version, archive as-of date, export date, counts, input hash, scope and caveats. Root `records` is an array:

- `id`: stable source-derived identifier; candidate and lead prefixes distinguish units.
- `kind`: programme, candidate or lead.
- `title`, `institution`, `country`, `field`: navigation labels. “Country” is a geographic research label and includes Hong Kong. Missing geography is Unspecified.
- `status`: archived research status, not current application-open status.
- `checkedAt`: latest recorded check date where available; null when unknown. It does not certify every field as current.
- `latestRegional`, `pureMaths`: scope flags.
- `sourceLinkStatus`: linked or not_resolved_in_scoped_archive.
- `sources`: public URLs and source-host labels. Links are evidence references, not a claim all pages remain accessible.
- `sections`: readable research prose, with label, text and historical verification status.
- `details`: structured research observations and conflicts. Dates, currencies, fee years, prerequisite gates and uncertainties are retained rather than converted into unsupported universal eligibility or total-cost figures.

Titles/institution labels are derived from archived records, with five missing institution labels filled from their preserved titles; no new admission facts are inferred. Known country aliases are normalized. Equal section objects are displayed once. Original database rows are untouched.

## SQLite

- `metadata(key, value_json)` stores the JSON metadata.
- `records(id, kind, title, institution, country, field, status, checked_at, latest_regional, pure_maths, source_link_status, details_json)` stores normalized record navigation and structured details.
- `sections(record_id, position, label, text, status)` preserves readable sections.
- `sources(record_id, position, url, title)` preserves links.

The export is generated by `scripts/export_catalogue.py` using a read-only connection to the private input database. `scripts/build_public_db.py` consumes only the public JSON. The input database is not included in this repository. Null, unverified and conflicting data are intentional and must not be filled with guesses.
