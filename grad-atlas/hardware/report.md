# Maths → real hardware: researched shortlist
**Checked 31 October 2026.** Singapore/Hong Kong first. This is an admissions-and-training shortlist, not a promise of employment. No pure-maths degrees are recommended.

## Decision
1. **For wafer production, process/yield and packaging: NUS Semiconductor Technology and Operations.** Mathematics explicitly qualifies; choose technical courses and a genuine engineering internship rather than an operations-only project.
2. **For GPU/TPU-adjacent digital hardware, FPGA and embedded work: NUS Computer Engineering, Computing Hardware Infrastructure.** Applied mathematics explicitly qualifies; a pure/general maths degree is not explicitly covered and needs individual assessment. This is the stronger digital-hardware route, not a fab-process qualification.
3. **For a genuine engineering conversion from maths with weak circuits/physics: BU LEAP → ECE**, if its extra time and substantial cost are acceptable.
4. **For Hong Kong: CityU P86 and P54 are conditional shortlist candidates**, not yet confirmed maths conversions. P86 better matches manufacturing; P54 offers an industrial research/internship structure.
5. **Purdue online** is explicitly maths-accessible, but use it for skills while building local experience—not as a substitute for relocating to a campus or doing cleanroom work.

The ordering is my judgement from admission certainty and curriculum/work-integrated structure. It does **not** infer a placement rate from a university ranking or employer logos.

## What “hardware” means here
- **Chip design / GPU–TPU architecture:** digital logic, RTL, verification, computer architecture, EDA and accelerator implementation. Maths can be particularly useful in formal verification, optimisation and numerical datapaths.
- **Semiconductor manufacturing / process / materials:** devices, solid-state physics, process integration, metrology, yield and production control. Statistics alone does not replace device/process knowledge.
- **Packaging / test:** heterogeneous integration, reliability, thermal/mechanical constraints, design-for-test and production test. Requires electronics and some materials/mechanics.
- **Embedded hardware:** FPGA/microcontroller systems, C/C++, firmware, interfaces, board-level debugging. This is real hardware work but is not the same as fabricating a leading-edge GPU.

## Employment evidence and preparation
A concrete employer example—not a hiring forecast—is NVIDIA's 2026 graduate formal-verification requisition JR2019447. It explicitly includes mathematics degrees, while demanding GPU/CPU architecture, C, Verilog/SystemVerilog, assertions and scripting. This demonstrates that maths-to-chip work exists **when engineering skills accompany the degree**. The posting may no longer be accepting applicants. [NVIDIA requisition](https://nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite/job/US-CA-Santa-Clara/Formal-Verification-Engineer---New-College-Grad-2026_JR2019447).

Suggested preparation (advisory, not a claimed university admission requirement):
- **Digital track:** digital logic → C/C++ → Verilog/SystemVerilog → architecture/cache/memory → FPGA project with tests and timing reports. Add formal proofs/assertions for a small arithmetic unit; document performance and correctness rather than only an AI demo.
- **Manufacturing track:** introductory circuits and semiconductor physics → fabrication process flow → statistical process control/design of experiments → a yield/metrology analysis with reproducible code. Seek hands-on device/process/packaging work during the degree.
- **Packaging/test:** circuits, basic heat transfer/materials, reliability statistics and test automation; produce an instrumented hardware project if equipment is available.
- **Application evidence:** map transcript modules to prerequisites; show engineering projects; state the exact job family rather than “GPU production.” Confirm conditional eligibility before paying non-refundable deposits.

## Work-rights constraints: nationality not assumed
**Singapore:** a degree is not automatic permission to work. For new Employment Pass applications from 1 January 2027, the non-financial minimum starts at S$6,000/month and rises with age; COMPASS ordinarily applies. Entry-level salaries and employer willingness matter. Other pass routes have their own rules and are not assessed here. [MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility).

**Hong Kong:** eligible nonlocal graduates of qualifying full-time locally accredited programmes can apply through IANG. Recent graduates applying within six months can ordinarily apply without a job offer and receive 24 months, subject to criteria. Nationality exclusions/restrictions exist, and the qualification normally requires at least one academic year. Do not assume every nationality or self-financed programme is covered. [Immigration Department](https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/services/visas/IANG.html).

**United States:** this report does not establish an individual's visa, internship or post-study work eligibility. BU should be evaluated with the student's actual status; online Purdue is not presented as an immigration pathway.
