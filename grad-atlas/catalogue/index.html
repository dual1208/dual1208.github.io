About the data

A map, not a verdict.

This catalogue brings together graduate-programme research for exploring fields beyond pure mathematics. Programme records and candidate observations are distinct: several records can describe the same programme, a different route, or a later reassessment.

Counts refer to research records, not unique programmes. Candidate observations are not confirmed offers or a determination of your eligibility.

Fees, dates and requirements are preserved as researched, including uncertainty and historical intake years. A checked date is a research timestamp, not a promise that information remains current. Confirm every application detail with its official source.

Shortlists are stored only in this browser. No account, analytics or server is used. Clearing browser storage removes your shortlist.
